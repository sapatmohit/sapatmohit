### `0001` &nbsp;[babelize](https://babelize.co)

An AI workspace for teams — connect MCP servers, build agents, keep prompts
and workflows and knowledge in one place instead of seven tabs.
I founded it. It is the thing I think about in the shower.

`TypeScript` · `Next.js` · `MCP` · shipping this week

### `0002` &nbsp;[babelize-elements](https://elements.babelize.co)

Copy-paste React and Tailwind components for localization — language
switchers, locale pickers, translation widgets. Open source, because
everyone rebuilds a language switcher and everyone does it badly.

`TypeScript` · `React` · `Tailwind` · open source, [source](https://github.com/babelize/babelize-elements)

### `0003` &nbsp;[tool-hive](https://github.com/sapatmohit/tool-hive)

A neighbourhood tool-lending library. Your neighbour owns a hammer drill
he uses twice a year — this is the software that admits that out loud.
Airbnb-shaped, hyper-local, no money changes hands.

`TypeScript` · `Bun` · `Next.js` · running

### `0004` &nbsp;[IRA_3.0](https://github.com/sapatmohit/IRA_3.0)

A digital assistant built to act *on behalf of* me — takes calls, drafts
mail, negotiates, and hands control back when it's out of its depth.
Modular agents, self-learning, absolutely a JARVIS problem.

`Python` · `LLM agents` · idle, not finished

### `0005` &nbsp;[SpeakNinja](https://github.com/sapatmohit/SpeakNinja)

Chatbot for practising spoken English. Flutter and Firebase, Gemini for
the talking, BERT for guessing how good you already are.
Hasn't needed a commit in over a year. The proudest kind of dead.

`Flutter` · `Dart` · `Firebase` · complete, not abandoned
